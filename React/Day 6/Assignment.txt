 3.which are the place did you use var,let,const in your point of viewvar

var is the older way of declaring variables in JavaScript. It has function scope and can be redeclared and reassigned.

It is mainly found in older JavaScript applications. In modern projects, I would generally avoid using var because let and const provide better block-level scope.
